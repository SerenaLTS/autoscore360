JAC Hunter PHEV Australia 2026: price, specs and towing

Quick answer: The JAC Hunter PHEV is now on sale in Australia in Pro and X pickup grades. Published launch prices are $49,988 for Pro and $54,844 for X, before on-road costs. Both have a 360kW plug-in hybrid drivetrain, a 31.2kWh battery, 3500kg braked towing and 915kg nominal payload. Dealer stock and delivery dates vary. The separately announced Pro Cab Chassis is still listed as coming soon.

Charging and mobile power

3) AC, DC and V2L

JAC lists 11kW AC charging (15–100% in about 3.5 hours) and up to 46kW DC charging (15–80% in about 30 minutes), using a CCS2 charging connection. Those times depend on the charger, battery temperature and conditions; a household outlet will be slower. Both grades support V2L. Confirm the approved adapter and output limit before planning campsite or tool loads: the brochure marks V2L as included but does not give a numeric output rating.

Safety and ownership

4) ANCAP and warranty

Hunter has a five-star ANCAP rating with a 2024 datestamp, applicable to all variants in ANCAP's listed scope. It draws on T9 testing plus additional Hunter crash tests for battery and high-voltage safety. Scores are 85% adult occupant protection, 87% child occupant protection, 87% vulnerable road user protection and 89% safety assist. ANCAP advises against child-restraint installation in the rear centre seat because it has no top tether anchorage. Read the ANCAP assessment and applicability.

JAC lists a seven-year/unlimited-kilometre vehicle warranty and an eight-year/150,000km battery warranty, with terms and exclusions. Seven-year capped-price servicing and roadside assistance are also advertised; capped-price servicing is not free servicing. Confirm the service schedule, battery conditions and commercial-use terms with your dealer. JAC ownership coverage.

Buyer fit

5) Who should shortlist it?

A useful fit when
  • You can charge at home or work regularly.
  • You want a lower entry price and can verify the equipment you need.
  • Your loaded towing combination fits all mass limits.
Investigate further when
  • You rarely charge or tow long distances near maximum weight.
  • You carry heavy accessories, passengers and tools together.
  • Long-term resale and ownership history are key priorities.
