SITRAK G5S Australia: cargo, tipper and mixer configurations

G5S is the broadest rigid and vocational series in SITRAK Australia's published range. It spans 4x2, 6x4 and 8x4 cargo trucks, 6x4 and 8x4 tippers, and an 8x4 mixer cab/chassis. Its Allison automatic transmissions and multiple axle layouts make the exact configuration the starting point for comparison.

Specifications checked 24 August 2026. Confirm the current Australian build sheet, completed tare, axle limits, availability and body compatibility before ordering.

Which G5S configuration should an operator inspect?

4x2 cargo

The 18,000kg-GVM starting point for operators who need rigid cargo capacity without a tandem-drive layout. Confirm body length, turning needs, loading method and completed rear-axle load.

6x4 cargo or tipper

The 25,000kg-GVM pathway for heavier rigid work. Cargo and tipper versions use different published output and transmission details, so specifications should not be combined.

8x4 cargo or tipper

The 31,000kg-GVM option provides more axle capacity for heavy body applications, subject to route, mass and dimensional requirements.

8x4 mixer

A cab/chassis pathway for agitator work. Drum, water, hydraulics, chute equipment and operational load must all be included in the finished mass calculation.

G5S buyer checklist

  • Request the exact Australian model code, axle layout and build sheet.
  • Obtain a body-builder tare estimate and axle-load calculation before comparing payload.
  • Confirm Allison transmission model, PTO requirements and cooling for the duty cycle.
  • Document servicing intervals, workshop access, parts pathways and warranty classification.
  • Compare delivered and registered vehicles, not cab/chassis headline prices.
