Update standard

Snapshot datasets

Recall and ANCAP tables state their coverage period. Their date describes the dataset, not necessarily the date you are reading the page.

Fast-changing information

PHEV status, pricing, availability and announced specifications are reviewed more often and carry a visible checked date where they affect the buying conclusion.

Status language

Models are labelled on sale, incoming or watchlist. Announced figures are kept separate from confirmed Australian production specifications.

Corrections

Material corrections update the affected page and are recorded in the public update log so readers can see what changed.

How to read the site

Scores are shortlist tools

Scores and rankings are designed to structure research, not replace inspection, test drives, finance checks, insurance quotes or professional advice.

PHEV claims need extra caution

Battery range, towing and payload are especially sensitive to test cycle, grade, load, accessories and driving pattern. Concept-stage models such as Chery KP31 should be treated as watchlist information until final Australian specifications arrive.

AI-curated summaries are labelled

Review pages organise public-source themes into readable buyer summaries. They are not road tests conducted by Auto Insight Lab by AutoScore360.

Corrections are welcome

If a source, specification or link is outdated, use the contact and corrections page so the site can be updated cleanly.
