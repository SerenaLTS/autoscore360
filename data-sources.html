Data sources, update rhythm and limits

Auto Insight Lab organises public information into buyer tools and summaries. This page explains the main source categories, where judgement is involved and where readers should verify details before making a purchase decision.

Main source categories

ANCAP ratings

Used for safety rating pages, sub-score comparisons and SafetyScore-style rankings. Tested year and protocol context remain important.

Recall notices

Used for recall search and recall interpretation articles. The current local CSV is a Jan 2026 snapshot, with the next refresh planned for late 2026 and then every six months. Recall count is not treated as a direct quality score.

How to read the site

Scores are shortlist tools

Scores and rankings are designed to structure research, not replace inspection, test drives, finance checks, insurance quotes or professional advice.

PHEV claims need extra caution

Battery range, towing and payload are especially sensitive to test cycle, grade, load, accessories and driving pattern. Concept-stage models such as Chery KP31 should be treated as watchlist information until final Australian specifications arrive.

AI-curated summaries are labelled

Review pages organise public-source themes into readable buyer summaries. They are not road tests conducted by Auto Insight Lab.

Corrections are welcome

If a source, specification or link is outdated, use the contact and corrections page so the site can be updated cleanly.
