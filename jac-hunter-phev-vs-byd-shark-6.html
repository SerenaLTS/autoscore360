Verdict

2) Which one makes more sense?

Shortlist Hunter PHEV if
  • You are price-sensitive and can wait for final Australian details.
  • You want maximum announced output and payload value.
  • You already see JAC T9 as a credible value ute but want plug-in hybrid hardware.
Shortlist Shark 6 if
  • You want an on-sale PHEV ute with more market visibility today.
  • You prefer BYD's wider electrification reputation.
  • You are comparing by grade and can justify the Performance model's capability upgrade.
Neutral read: Hunter PHEV is the more disruptive story; Shark 6 is the safer comparison anchor today. A buyer should not treat Hunter as settled until final pricing, equipment, warranty detail and road tests land.
