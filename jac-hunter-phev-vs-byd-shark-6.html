Loaded towing

What does Hunter’s 3500kg rating leave for passengers and gear?

Hunter’s brochure lists 2555kg kerb mass, 3470kg GVM, 6430kg GCM and a maximum 350kg towball download. Here is an illustrative mass budget for a trailer with a loaded ATM of 3500kg and 350kg on the ball:

StepIllustrative calculation
Trailer load on its axles3500 − 350 = 3150kg
Ute mass ceiling from GCM6430 − 3150 = 3280kg, including the towball load
Remaining for people, accessories and cargo3280 − 2555 − 350 = 375kg

In this example, GCM restricts the ute before its 3470kg GVM is reached. Four occupants totalling 300kg would leave 75kg for accessories and cargo. The 350kg ball load is an example, not a recommended setting for every trailer. Avoid counting it twice in the combination mass.

Actual kerb mass, axle loads, towbar limits and the trailer’s own limits still apply. Weigh the fitted, loaded combination. Use our payload and towing calculator and towing guide to plan the checks; run a separate calculation for the selected Shark 6 grade.

Daily ownership

Charging access matters more than a small battery-size difference

JAC publishes 11kW AC and 46kW DC charging for Hunter, with claimed times of 3.5 hours from 15–100% on AC and around 30 minutes from 15–80% on DC under suitable conditions. Your actual supply, charger and battery conditions affect the result; a normal household socket does not deliver 11kW.

Ask both dealers to identify the exact vehicle’s charging limits, included cables and home-installation requirements. Compare electricity cost using energy drawn from the wall, including charging losses. Our Hunter charging-cost examples show the assumptions; the wallbox guide helps assess an overnight setup.

JAC confirms Hunter can operate without plugging in, with the engine managing battery charge. That establishes usability, not a fuel-saving guarantee. If you cannot charge regularly, compare engine-on running costs with a diesel using your own driving pattern. Published PHEV combined fuel figures are not an empty-battery towing forecast.

Sources: JAC model page and JAC charging FAQ.

Buying questions

JAC Hunter vs BYD Shark 6 FAQ

Which is cheaper, Hunter or Shark 6?

On the listed price references above, Hunter Pro starts below Shark 6 Premium. These are not equivalent-equipment driveaway quotes. Include on-road costs, paint, tow equipment, accessories and any charging installation before deciding.

Can both tow 3500kg?

Hunter Pro and X and Shark 6 Performance have published 3500kg braked ratings. Shark 6 Premium is rated to 2500kg. Each vehicle must also meet GVM, GCM, axle, towball and towbar limits with your actual load.

Does Hunter X carry or tow more than Pro?

No. Both publish 915kg nominal payload and 3500kg braked towing. X changes equipment; fitted accessories and people still consume the available payload.

Can I buy a Hunter without a home charger?

Yes, JAC says plugging in is not required for driving. Check whether reliable public or workplace charging fits your routine, and budget for engine-on use. You cannot assume the brochure’s PHEV combined consumption will describe that routine.

Which is better for a family?

Try your child seats, rear-seat access and parking routine in each exact grade. ANCAP advises against fitting a child restraint in Hunter’s rear centre position because it has no top tether anchorage. Check the Hunter ANCAP assessment and each vehicle’s handbook for installation details.

Which is more reliable?

This specification comparison cannot establish a long-term winner. Warranty length is not a failure-rate measure. Check service access, warranty exclusions and model-specific owner evidence using our ute reliability guide.
