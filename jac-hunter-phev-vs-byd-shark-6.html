Verdict

2) Which one makes more sense?

Shortlist Hunter PHEV if
  • You are price-sensitive and can compare a Pro or X driveaway quote.
  • You want 3500kg braked towing and can stay within the loaded mass limits.
  • You already see JAC T9 as a credible value ute but want plug-in hybrid hardware.
Shortlist Shark 6 if
  • You want an on-sale PHEV ute with more market visibility today.
  • You prefer BYD's wider electrification reputation.
  • You are comparing by grade and can justify the Performance model's capability upgrade.
Neutral read: Hunter PHEV is the more disruptive story; Shark 6 is the safer comparison anchor today. Hunter now has published prices, equipment and warranty information; long-term reliability and resale remain developing evidence.
