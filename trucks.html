Australian heavy commercial vehicle guide

Heavy truck brands in Australia

The right heavy truck starts with the work, combination and support plan—not the badge. Australia has Japanese, European, American and Chinese brands competing across heavy rigid, tipper, mixer, prime-mover, line-haul and vocational applications. This hub compares their range starting points without declaring one universal winner.

Reviewed 24 August 2026. Brand ranges, dealer territories and specifications change; confirm the exact model, mass limits, completed configuration and support arrangements with the manufacturer and an authorised dealer.

A model appearing in a row is only a research starting point. Not every variant is approved or suitable for every body, trailer, route or mass scheme.

Start with the truck class

Light-duty trucks

Often used for local delivery, landscaping, trades, rentals and compact tippers. Check GVM, licence class, access, payload after the body is fitted and urban safety.

Medium-duty trucks

Suited to larger distribution, civil, waste and vocational work. Axle layout, body mass, route profile, turning circle and downtime support become more important.

Heavy-duty trucks

Used for line-haul, prime-mover, tipper, mixer and heavy vocational work. Compare GCM, engine and transmission fit, fuel use, service coverage and driver environment.

How to choose a truck brand and model

Define the operating job

Record freight or material, routes, annual kilometres, gradients, stop-start use, operating hours and expected replacement cycle.

Build the finished truck on paper

Compare the completed body, accessories, passengers, fuel and payload—not an incomplete cab-chassis headline.

Map service and parts support

Ask who can service the exact model, workshop hours, mobile support, parts lead times and what happens after a breakdown.

Model downtime and resale

Purchase price is only one input. Include finance, fuel, AdBlue or energy, tyres, scheduled maintenance, unplanned downtime and disposal value.

Check compliance and body fit

Confirm GVM, GCM, axle limits, dimensions, body-builder requirements and the rules applying to the intended operation.

Compare like with like

Request written quotes for equivalent bodies, specifications, warranty conditions, service plans and delivery timing.
