Hunter Pro vs X: spend where it matters

Hunter Pro is the logical starting point for value. Hunter X is worth a closer look when you want the extra camera assistance, heated seats and standard differential locks. The key is that both pickups retain the same core PHEV performance and load ratings.

Practical

The published launch price gap is $4,856 before on-road costs. Choose the equipment you will use; the X does not buy a larger battery or a higher towing limit.

2) Why Pro is an appealing entry point

Pro keeps the drivetrain, battery and headline towing capability that make Hunter interesting in the first place. It is therefore our preferred place to start for a buyer whose priorities are plug-in commuting and general ute use, rather than a particular comfort package.

Build a list of essentials before adding options. A charger installation, the correct towing equipment or cargo protection may be more useful to your routine than cosmetic upgrades. Get each item priced separately. There is no need to spend the entire grade difference simply because it is available.

3) When X earns its extra cost

X makes the clearest sense when its extra equipment solves problems you encounter often. A 360-degree camera can help you judge the space around a pickup in tight parking areas, while heated seats may be a welcome daily feature in colder weather. Camera assistance still requires the driver to check the surroundings.

For buyers who specifically want front and rear differential locks, compare an appropriately optioned Pro against X rather than comparing the two base list prices alone. Ask the dealer to confirm the Pro option’s price and availability in writing. We have not assumed an option price or claimed that X always works out cheaper.

5) What the upgrade does not change

Both grades share the published 360kW output, 31.2kWh battery, 3500kg braked towing and 915kg nominal payload. X does not create extra capacity for a heavy caravan or accessories. Use the mass limits in our Hunter specification guide for either grade.

Compare matching body styles and the same quote date. The announced Pro Cab Chassis is a separate upcoming version and should not be treated as the on-sale Pro pickup. Finish with a test drive and a written order identifying the exact equipment.

Our pick: Pro for the clearest value proposition; X for buyers who will regularly benefit from its equipment. Both offer a convincing route into the Hunter PHEV platform.
