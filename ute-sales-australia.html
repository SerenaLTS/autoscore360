What changed in June?

Ranger and HiLux still led YTD

Ranger remained first among the listed utes at 26,314 YTD, ahead of HiLux at 22,607.

Shark 6 had a large June

BYD Shark 6 delivered 3,398 units in June, making it the third-highest ute result for the month in this table, while remaining fifth YTD.

GWM and BT-50 were almost level YTD

Only eight deliveries separated GWM Cannon (6,161) and Mazda BT-50 (6,153) through June.

JAC’s evidence remains limited

JAC T9 recorded 437 deliveries YTD. That does not prove poor mechanical quality, but it means a smaller installed base and less Australian market evidence.

How AutoScore360 uses sales

Sales are one market signal, not a verdict. High volume can improve parts familiarity, used-market evidence and confidence that a model has been tested by a broad owner base. It can also reflect fleet contracts, supply, incentives and brand strength. It does not prove safety, durability or buyer suitability.

The Ute Calculator keeps Sales separate from Safety, Economy, Performance and Ownership Confidence. Users can reduce the sales weighting to zero. The current score model uses broad sales tiers rather than inserting this monthly table directly into the score.
