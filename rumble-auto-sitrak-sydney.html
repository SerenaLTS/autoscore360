Servicing, parts and fleet support

RUMBLE Auto publishes support for maintenance and repairs, genuine parts, fleet programs and technical enquiries. Start through the official service and parts page.

  • Service booking: provide VIN, model, odometer, operating hours where recorded, duty cycle and the required date.
  • Parts enquiry: include VIN, part number if known, photographs and the delivery location.
  • Diagnostics: describe the symptoms, warning messages and when the issue occurs.
  • Fleet planning: explain truck numbers, shift schedules, workshop windows and record-keeping requirements.

Confirm booking availability, parts lead times and any external support arrangements for your routes. Do not assume round-the-clock coverage, a replacement vehicle or a particular repair turnaround without an agreed arrangement.

What a useful written quote should cover

Request the exact chassis and axle configuration, body or trailer compatibility, finished-mass estimate, included equipment, tax treatment, delivery and registration inclusions. Identify who handles body installation and final handover.

For warranty, ask which vehicle-use category applies, what is covered, the time and distance limits, servicing obligations and the claims process. Read the current manufacturer warranty summary alongside the purchase contract.
