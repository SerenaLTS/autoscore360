Payload guide

GWM Cannon Alpha PHEV payload explained: the number behind the 3500kg tow claim

The Cannon Alpha PHEV is compelling because it combines plug-in hybrid power, a large battery, low-range 4WD hardware, V2L and a 3500kg braked towing claim. The trade-off buyers need to read carefully is payload.

Last checked: 7 July 2026. Confirm current payload, GVM, GCM and towball limits on the exact grade before buying.

Why payload matters

Towing capacity is not the whole carrying story. Payload is consumed by people, accessories, luggage, tray load, canopy, tools, bull bar, fuel, towball download and sometimes dealer-fit extras. A 3500kg tow rating can still leave a tight real-world carrying budget.

Battery weight

PHEV utes carry battery hardware. That can help performance and electric driving, but it also affects mass planning.

Towball download

A heavy trailer can place hundreds of kilograms on the vehicle. That weight usually comes out of the payload allowance.

Accessories

Canopy, drawers, roof rack, bull bar and long-range touring gear can use payload before the first passenger climbs in.

Family touring

Four occupants, luggage and a caravan can be a harder test than the brochure tow figure suggests.
