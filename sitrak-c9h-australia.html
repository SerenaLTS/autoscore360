SITRAK C9H Australia: 660hp, 90-tonne-GCM prime mover

The Australian-listed SITRAK C9H is a 6x4 heavy-duty prime mover publishing 660hp, 3200Nm and a 90,000kg GCM. It is the high-output flagship above the 540hp G7S, aimed at demanding combination and long-haul applications where route, trailer, support and whole-of-life assumptions must be assessed together.

Specifications checked 24 August 2026. Confirm current Australian stock, specification, applicable mass approvals, warranty and completed combination before purchase.

Where the C9H fits

More output than G7S

C9H publishes 660hp and 3200Nm, compared with 540hp and 2550Nm for the listed G7S prime mover. The practical value depends on gross mass, gradients, average speed, fuel use and utilisation.

Long-haul driver environment

Public Australian listings describe a sleeper cab, driver-assistance equipment and long-haul comfort features. Confirm the exact delivered equipment because stock specifications can differ.

Safety specification

Public listings reference systems such as adaptive cruise control, emergency braking, forward-collision and lane-departure warnings. Verify calibration, operating limits and the exact build sheet.

Whole-of-life evidence

Purchase price and power are only part of a prime-mover decision. Model fuel, tyres, service intervals, workshop access, parts, downtime, driver acceptance and resale.

C9H warranty starting point

SITRAK Australia currently publishes three years or 600,000km cab-and-chassis warranty and three years or 1,000,000km driveline warranty for prime movers and rigid trucks, whichever occurs first. Consumables and policy conditions apply.

Obtain the current warranty document and clarify scheduled servicing, authorised repair processes, consequential loss, towing/recovery and configuration-specific exclusions.
