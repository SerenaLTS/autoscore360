Where Victoria's main truck dealer clusters are

Dandenong South and Keysborough

A strong southeast starting point for Isuzu, Hino, Daimler Truck brands and Scania. Compare the exact sales franchise and workshop capability rather than assuming every nearby site supports every model.

Derrimut and Laverton

Melbourne's west provides Hino, Kenworth, DAF, Volvo, Mack, UD and Scania options close to major freight corridors and industrial areas.

Pakenham and Gippsland

Pakenham adds a southeast Isuzu sales and service point, while Traralgon provides a regional Volvo Group starting point. Other Gippsland outlets should be confirmed through brand locators.

Ballarat, Bendigo and other regional centres

Regional operators should map both the sales dealer and the workshop that will handle scheduled maintenance, urgent repairs and parts. Hino and Isuzu publish additional Victorian locations beyond those highlighted here.

Match the dealer to the truck's job

Metro delivery and trade

Compare licence class, cab width, turning circle, urban safety, automatic transmission and usable payload after the body is fitted.

Construction and vocational work

Bring the body-builder requirements, PTO needs, axle loads, ground clearance and site-access constraints into the dealer discussion.

Regional and line-haul

Model route, GCM, driver accommodation, fuel assumptions, maintenance intervals, roadside coverage and the nearest suitable workshop.

Fleet replacement

Ask for comparable finished-vehicle quotes, delivery timing, service capacity, parts support and a written escalation process for downtime.
