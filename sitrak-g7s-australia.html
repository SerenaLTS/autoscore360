SITRAK G7S Australia: 540hp prime mover and tipper range

G7S sits above G5S in SITRAK Australia's heavy-duty range. The published 6x4 prime mover pairs 540hp with a 90,000kg GCM rating, while 6x4 and 8x4 tipper configurations publish 75,000kg GCM. Route, combination, axle layout and body determine which version is relevant.

Specifications checked 24 August 2026. Verify the exact Australian configuration, mass approvals, fifth-wheel or body installation, delivery status and contract specifications.

Prime mover or tipper?

G7S 6x4 prime mover

The 90-tonne published GCM and 540hp output place this configuration in heavy combination work. Compare tare, fuel capacity, wheelbase, fifth-wheel position, trailer pairing and the actual route.

G7S 6x4 tipper

A tandem-drive vocational starting point. Body volume should not be confused with legal payload; calculate tare and axle distribution for the material carried.

G7S 8x4 tipper

The 31-tonne published GVM adds rigid axle capacity. Site access, turning, tyre wear, body mass and relevant mass schemes still determine productivity.

What a G7S quote should identify

  • Exact 6x4 or 8x4 model, Australian compliance and build date.
  • Engine output, ZF transmission specification, axle ratios and retarder details.
  • Finished tare, axle loads, fifth-wheel or tipper-body specification.
  • Current warranty terms and whether the operation is classified as construction/off-road use.
  • Service location, parts pathway, roadside response and delivery timing.
