PHEV ute towing battle: 2500kg vs 3500kg in Australia

PHEV ute towing is no longer one simple number. JAC Hunter PHEV is one of the key 3500kg watchlist models because its announced package combines 3500kg braked towing with a 915kg payload claim. For buyers who want a JAC ute today, JAC T9 remains the current diesel option, with a lower 3200kg braked towing figure but a more immediate ownership story. BYD Shark 6 now splits between 2500kg and 3500kg depending on grade, while GWM Cannon Alpha PHEV and Ford Ranger PHEV target the traditional 3500kg benchmark.

Last checked: 23 June 2026. Always confirm final braked towing, GVM, GCM, payload and towball limits in the current brochure before buying.

Quick answer

If you need a PHEV ute with a 3500kg braked towing claim, include JAC Hunter PHEV on the shortlist alongside BYD Shark 6 Performance, GWM Cannon Alpha PHEV, Ford Ranger PHEV and the incoming Chery Stockman target. If you are looking at BYD Shark 6 Dynamic or Premium, the published towing number is 2500kg.

Why 3500kg is not the whole answer

Towball download

A heavy trailer can put hundreds of kilograms on the towball. That weight usually comes out of payload.

Payload after accessories

Bullbar, canopy, drawers, people, tools and water can consume payload before the trailer is attached.

Range while towing

PHEV electric range can shrink sharply under load. Count fuel use, not just EV range.

Charging pattern

A PHEV that starts each day charged behaves differently from one used as an uncharged petrol hybrid ute.
