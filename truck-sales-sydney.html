Choose a Sydney truck dealer by the work you need done

Urban delivery and trade

Prioritise dimensions, turning circle, licence class, automatic transmission, visibility, payload after body fit and workshop access close to the operating area.

Construction and vocational

Compare axle configuration, ground clearance, PTO and body-builder requirements, site access, payload, GVM and parts support for high-wear components.

Line-haul and prime mover

Model route, GCM, fuel use, driver accommodation, safety systems, maintenance intervals, roadside support and replacement-vehicle risk.

Information to prepare before requesting a truck quote

Load and application

Describe the goods or material, typical and maximum payload, body type, towing needs and loading method.

Route and utilisation

Provide annual kilometres, operating hours, metro or regional split, gradients, stop frequency and expected time in service.

Finished specification

Request a quote showing chassis, body, accessories, axle loads, tare estimate, usable payload, GVM and GCM.

Ownership support

Ask for written warranty conditions, service pricing, intervals, workshop hours, roadside process and likely parts availability.

Commercial terms

Separate vehicle price, body cost, on-road charges, finance, trade-in, delivery date and any conditional fleet discount.

Comparison discipline

Compare equivalent finished vehicles and support packages. A lower cab-chassis price may not mean a lower operating cost.
