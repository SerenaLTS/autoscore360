Worked towing scenario

Best ute for towing a 2500kg caravan

Short answer: mainstream Ranger, D-MAX, HiLux, Triton, BT-50, Tasman and Amarok grades with a 3,500kg rating can all place a 2,500kg caravan inside the headline tow limit. The better tow vehicle is the exact grade that still has enough payload and GCM headroom after occupants, accessories, luggage and towball download are counted.

Last checked: 30 July 2026. This example is educational and does not certify any combination. Weigh the completed vehicle and caravan.

What the example reveals

The caravan is not the hard part—the ute load is

An 815kg example load can fit a lighter work-oriented dual cab with healthy payload, yet leave little or no buffer in a heavy luxury or electrified grade. That does not make every lower-payload ute unsuitable; it means the buyer must remove accessories, reduce cargo, select another grade or change the towing setup.

Strong mainstream starting points

Ranger, D-MAX, HiLux, Triton and BT-50 4x4 dual-cab grades deserve the first calculation. Do not carry a maximum cab-chassis payload figure across to a heavier pick-up.

Kia Tasman

Its 6,200kg listed GCM and up-to-1,000kg payload proposition are useful, but grade and accessory mass still determine the result. Kia's own calculator shows how quickly a bullbar, canopy and tow kit add weight.

PHEV warning

Cannon Alpha PHEV's listed 685kg payload would be consumed by this 815kg example before the calculation reaches GCM. Shark 6 and Ranger PHEV outcomes depend on the grade; never assume the diesel figure applies.

Four legal limits

A valid setup must pass every test

Braked towing capacity

The loaded trailer must not exceed the vehicle, towbar or other applicable rating.

GVM

The loaded ute—including towball download—must stay within Gross Vehicle Mass.

GCM

The loaded vehicle and trailer combination must remain within the applicable Gross Combined Mass definition and limit.

Axle, tyre and towball limits

A combination can pass the broad totals yet overload an axle, tyre or coupling. Load distribution matters.

Before buying

Take this worksheet to the dealer

  1. Choose the exact body, drivetrain and grade—not only the model name.
  2. Record its tare/kerb mass basis, GVM, GCM, braked tow rating and maximum towball download.
  3. Add dealer-fitted accessories before delivery.
  4. Use realistic occupant and cargo masses.
  5. Obtain the caravan's loaded ATM, GTM and actual ball load.
  6. Confirm the completed combination on certified scales.
