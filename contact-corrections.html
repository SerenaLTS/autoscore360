Contact & Corrections

Auto Insight Lab is built from public data, published sources and editorial organisation. If something needs review, this page explains the best way to send it through.

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Email: autoscore360@gmail.com

What to Send

For corrections, source updates, brand concerns, rights questions or takedown requests, please include enough context to review the issue quickly.

  • The page URL or page title.
  • The exact statement, figure, source link or section you want reviewed.
  • The reason it may be inaccurate, outdated, incomplete or rights-sensitive.
  • A preferred correction, clarification, updated source or supporting reference where available.

Correction Review

Reasonable requests may result in amendment, clarification, source-link updates, page notes or removal where appropriate. Auto Insight Lab may also keep a page unchanged if the existing wording is supportable and clearly framed.

Data and Calculator Issues

If you notice a calculator, ANCAP table or recall result that appears wrong, include the vehicle, year, source record and expected value. Data pages often depend on source datasets, so exact references help a lot.

General Enquiries

General site feedback, broken links and suggestions for future comparison pages can also be sent to the same email address. This site does not provide personalised vehicle, legal, financial or safety advice.
