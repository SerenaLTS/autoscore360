Isuzu range starting points

N and F Series

N Series is the light-duty starting point, while F Series extends into medium-duty distribution, trade and vocational work.

FX and FY Series

FX and FY cover heavier rigid and application-specific pathways where axle layout, GVM, GCM and body engineering become central.

Series names group many wheelbases, axle layouts, engines, transmissions and cab configurations. Compare the exact Australian variant and completed vehicle.

What to check before requesting a quote

Fit the operating job

Compare finished tare and payload, licence implications, wheelbase, axle loads, transmission, Ready-to-Work body specifications and the capacity of the servicing branch.

Price the finished truck

Compare equivalent bodies, accessories, registration class, payload, warranty, service plan and delivery timing—not incomplete cab-chassis prices.

Map support and downtime

Confirm which branch can perform warranty work, workshop hours, mobile support, parts lead times and the contingency after a breakdown.
