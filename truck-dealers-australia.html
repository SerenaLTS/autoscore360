Choose a dealer by the support you need

New-truck sales

Confirm the branch is authorised for the exact brand and can quote the completed truck—not only a bare cab chassis.

Service and warranty

Ask whether the workshop can perform scheduled work, software updates and warranty repairs on the exact model and completed body.

Parts and downtime

Check critical local stock, after-hours contacts, mobile service, towing or recovery and parts lead times from another state.

Used-truck inspection

Separate the seller from an independent inspection. Verify service history, recalls, emissions equipment, body condition and finance status.

What to prepare before contacting a truck dealer

  • Freight, body or trailer type, typical and maximum load, and required payload.
  • Annual kilometres, route, gradients, stop-start use, site access and operating hours.
  • Required GVM, GCM, axle arrangement, licence limits and any road-access constraints.
  • Body, tail lift, crane, PTO, hydraulics, safety equipment, fuel, driver and accessories.
  • Required delivery date, finance basis, warranty, service plan and replacement horizon.

Request comparable written quotes showing completed tare, estimated payload and axle loads. A lower cab-chassis price can disappear after body, compliance, delivery and downtime requirements are included.
