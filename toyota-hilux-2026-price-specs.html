Toyota HiLux guide

Toyota HiLux 2026 price and specs: what changed and what to check

The 2026 HiLux range is broader than a simple diesel workhorse story. Toyota lists WorkMate, SR, SR5, Rogue and Rugged X, with diesel, V-Active mild-hybrid assistance on selected grades and Battery Electric options on selected SR/SR5 body styles.

Last checked: 7 July 2026. Toyota driveaway pricing varies by location and may require postcode/dealer confirmation.

Price logic

Do not treat one national price as final. Toyota's public page displays driveaway pricing through a location-aware tool, and notes that prices can vary by state taxes, levies and regulations. Use the Toyota build/price flow or dealer quote for the final number.

WorkMate

The commercial entry point, offered across cab-chassis and pick-up body styles depending on drivetrain. Best for fleets and cost-sensitive buyers.

SR

The practical upgrade. Toyota lists extra-cab and double-cab options, with diesel 4x4 and selected Battery Electric choices.

SR5

The family/touring grade. SR5 is the natural private-buyer step because it adds comfort and technology while keeping the HiLux identity intact.

Rogue and Rugged X

Rogue is the premium adventure angle; Rugged X is the more off-road-styled flagship path.
