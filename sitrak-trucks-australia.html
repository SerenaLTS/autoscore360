Australian heavy-truck guide

SITRAK trucks Australia: G5S, G7S and C9H explained

SITRAK's Australian range is centred on heavy cargo, tipper, mixer and prime-mover applications. G5S covers rigid cargo and vocational configurations, G7S moves into 540hp prime-mover and tipper work, and C9H is the 660hp, 90-tonne-GCM long-haul flagship. The exact axle layout and Australian specification matter more than the series name alone.

Information checked 24 August 2026 against SITRAK Australia and publicly listed NSW dealer information. Confirm current compliance, availability, body allowance, warranty and pricing in writing before ordering.

Positioning

What is SITRAK?

Heavy commercial focus

SITRAK is a SINOTRUK brand. In Australia, the published range is focused on rigid cargo trucks, tippers, mixers and prime movers rather than light trucks or ute-sized commercial vehicles.

Configuration-led buying

A G5S 4x2 cargo truck and a G5S 8x4 mixer share a series name but do different work. Compare axle layout, body, tare estimate, GVM, GCM, engine output, transmission and route before comparing price.

Australian support

SITRAK Australia publishes national dealer and customer-support pathways. For Sydney and NSW, RUMBLE Auto in Arndell Park publicly identifies as an authorised SITRAK NSW dealer supporting sales, servicing, parts and fleet enquiries.

Evidence still developing

Specifications and warranty can be checked now; long-term Australian whole-of-life cost, resale and downtime evidence will develop as the local fleet grows. Buyers should request written support and parts arrangements.

Warranty and support

Published Australian warranty starting point

SITRAK Australia currently publishes a cab-and-chassis warranty of three years or 600,000km for prime movers and rigid trucks, with a driveline warranty of three years or 1,000,000km, whichever occurs first. Construction and off-road vehicles are listed with three years or 600,000km for both cab/chassis and driveline.

Warranty eligibility, exclusions, consumables, servicing requirements and the classification of a completed vehicle must be confirmed against the current policy and purchase contract.

Buyer checks

What to ask before requesting a SITRAK quote

Completed tare and payload

Request an estimated finished tare with body, hydraulics, fuel, driver and accessories, plus axle-by-axle load calculations.

Route and duty cycle

Provide annual kilometres, gradients, stop-start use, site conditions, mass requirements and expected operating hours.

Workshop and parts plan

Confirm the servicing location, workshop hours, mobile support, key parts availability, warranty escalation and expected downtime response.

Like-for-like quote

Compare completed trucks with equivalent body, safety equipment, tyres, warranty, service plan, registration and delivery timing.
