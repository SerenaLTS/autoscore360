2026 Australian towing guide

Best utes for towing in Australia

Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-MAX, Mitsubishi Triton and Toyota HiLux are the strongest mainstream places to start for regular caravan or work-trailer towing. The right winner depends on the exact grade and loaded combination—not the 3,500kg headline alone. Ranger Super Duty sits in a heavier-duty class, while Ford Ranger PHEV is the most straightforward PHEV starting point for buyers who still require a 3,500kg claim.

Last checked: 27 July 2026. Specifications vary by grade, body style, drivetrain, accessories and tow kit. Confirm the current Australian brochure and weigh the finished vehicle before towing.

The numbers most towing lists miss

Payload includes towball download

A 300kg towball load normally consumes about 300kg of payload before passengers, a canopy, drawers, tools, water or luggage are counted.

GCM can reduce usable capacity

A vehicle can be under GVM and a trailer under ATM while the combined rig still exceeds GCM.

Grade choice changes the answer

Luxury equipment, batteries and accessories add kerb mass. A work grade can retain more payload than the flagship.

Maximum is not a comfort target

Legal capacity does not guarantee relaxed performance, suitable axle loading or practical touring range.

Best ute for towing FAQ

Which ute is best for towing a caravan?

Ranger, D-MAX, Triton and eligible HiLux grades are strong mainstream starting points. Match the exact ute to the caravan’s loaded mass, then check remaining payload, GVM, GCM and towball download.

Which utes can tow 3500kg?

Many eligible Ranger, D-MAX, Triton and HiLux variants publish maximum braked ratings of up to 3,500kg. Ranger PHEV also targets this figure. Confirm the exact Australian grade and tow-kit requirements.

Does towball weight reduce payload?

Yes. Towball download normally counts toward payload alongside people, accessories and cargo. A 300kg towball load can therefore use about 300kg of available vehicle payload.
