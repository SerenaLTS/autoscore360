How Queensland truck support is distributed

Brisbane west

Wacol, Darra and Richlands form a strong heavy-truck starting point across Volvo, Mack, UD, Kenworth, DAF and Scania. Compare workshop hours, B-double access and the branch that owns the warranty relationship.

Brisbane south and Gold Coast corridor

Springwood provides a SITRAK sales starting point, with City Fleet publishing related workshop locations in Underwood, Nathan and Molendinar. Confirm where each model's service and parts work is performed.

Regional Queensland

Toowoomba, Rockhampton, Mackay, Townsville and Cairns support different freight, mining, agriculture and civil routes. Map the workshop network along the operating corridor rather than only the selling dealer.

Before requesting a Queensland truck quote

  • Provide the route, annual kilometres, mass, trailer or body and expected operating hours.
  • Ask whether the location supplies sales, full workshop service, parts, roadside support or only selected maintenance.
  • Confirm the completed tare, axle loads, GVM/GCM, delivery timing and body-builder responsibility.
  • Document the nearest suitable workshop for every regular regional route.
